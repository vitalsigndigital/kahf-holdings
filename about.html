The Founder

Asfand Khan builds companies the slow way.

E-commerce strategist. Operator. Philanthropist. Founder of KAHF — a Canadian house of ventures built one client, one product, and one promise at a time.

The Story

From one Amazon account to a house of companies.

Asfand started where most e-commerce careers do — inside the machinery of Amazon itself: listings, ads, suspensions, logistics. The difference was what he did with that knowledge.

Rather than staying a freelancer, he built eCommerceWing into a full-service agency trusted by brands across Canada and the United States, with client results like 600% growth and top-100 best-seller rankings. The playbooks that worked for clients became the foundation for the next arms of the house: Vital Sign Digital, applying the same performance discipline to local lead generation, and KAHF Brands, putting the house's own capital behind its own products.

“Agencies taught me how businesses grow. Holding them taught me why they last.”

The Path

How the house was assembled

  1. Chapter One

    The Operator Years

    Deep inside Amazon's marketplace — launching products, managing PPC, fighting suspensions, learning logistics across Canada and the US. The apprenticeship that everything else stands on.

  2. Chapter Two

    eCommerceWing

    The consultancy becomes an agency. Full-service Amazon account management, 3PL fulfillment, and seller education — with flagship client outcomes of 600% growth and 20× product-line increases.

  3. Chapter Three

    Vital Sign Digital

    The same accountability, aimed at Main Street. A lead-generation and marketing firm for GTA practices — dentists, med spas, law firms — measured on booked appointments, not vanity metrics.

  4. Chapter Four

    KAHF Brands & the Holding

    The house buys its own inventory. Consumer products — led by the premium KAHF Mahjong line — and the formation of KAHF as the holding company that binds the ventures together.

The Philosophy

What the founder believes

I.

Skin in the game

Advice is cheap until your own inventory is on the line. KAHF sells with its own capital what it recommends to clients.

II.

Results, not reports

Growth percentages, booked appointments, show rates. Every KAHF company is judged on numbers a client can bank.

III.

Local roots, wide reach

Headquartered in Toronto, serving two countries. Canadian in temperament — steady, direct, understated.

IV.

Keep what you build

The goal was never a portfolio of exits. It is a shelf of companies still worth owning in twenty years.

Beyond Business

Truman Foundation

Saving lives, rebuilding communities.

Business builds wealth; what matters is what wealth builds next. Asfand Khan is Co-Founder and Director of Truman Foundation, a Canadian registered charity headquartered in Scarborough, Ontario.

The foundation delivers humanitarian aid and future-oriented development programs designed to help families escape the poverty spiral for good — not just survive it. Last year its work reached 150,000 people across 20 countries.
